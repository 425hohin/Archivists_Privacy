Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Welcome to Archivists. We (the development team) take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy is designed to transparently explain how we handle your data when you use the Archivists application (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). Our core principle is: your reading data and notes belong entirely to you.

1. Data Collection and Storage

Archivists is a privacy-focused, local-first application. To ensure the App functions properly, all content you create, including but not limited to:

  • Book data (title, author, ISBN, etc.) and custom cover images
  • Reading records and progress tracking
  • Reflections, notes, and personal tags
  • In-app preferences

Storage Location: All the above data is saved directly on your local iOS device by default. If you enable the iCloud sync feature, this data will be encrypted, backed up, and synced to your personal Apple iCloud Private Database (CloudKit) via Apple's highly secure transmission protocols. We do not own, rent, or use any third-party servers to store your personal data.

2. Data Usage and Access

All data you store in the CloudKit Private Database is completely private:

  • Only you can access and sync it by logging into a device with the same Apple ID.
  • The development team (we) has absolutely no ability to read, access, intercept, or modify any of your personal reading records and notes.
  • We do not require you to create an Archivists account, nor do we collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

3. Device Permissions

To provide a complete core experience, the App may request the following device permissions, strictly limited to the operation of the respective features:

  • Camera Permission: Used exclusively to scan book ISBN barcodes. Image processing is performed in real-time locally on your device. We do not record video, nor do we upload or store images to any external servers.
  • Notifications Permission: Used exclusively to provide your custom "local reading reminders." These schedules operate independently on your device and are not pushed via external servers.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We guarantee that we will never sell, rent, or share any of your data with third-party companies, advertisers, or data analytics organizations. The App does not contain any third-party advertising tracking SDKs. Since data synchronization relies on Apple's iCloud services, data transmission and storage in this regard are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You have 100% control over your data. You can delete individual notes or books within the App at any time. If you wish to completely delete all data:

  • You can uninstall the App from your iOS device, which will delete all related local data.
  • To delete your iCloud backup, go to "Settings" > [Your Name] > "iCloud" > "Manage Account Storage" on your iOS device, find Archivists, and choose to delete the data.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or the relevant age according to local laws). Since we do not collect any user account data, if a parent or guardian discovers that a child has used the App and wishes to clear their local data, simply uninstalling the App from the device is sufficient.

7. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any significant changes will be updated on this page, and the "Last Updated" date at the top will be modified. We recommend that you review this policy periodically to stay informed about our latest privacy protection measures.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or need further assistance, please feel free to contact us via email:

隱私權政策 (Privacy Policy)

最後更新日期：2026 年 7 月

歡迎使用 Archivists。我們（開發團隊）非常重視您的隱私權。本隱私權政策旨在透明地說明我們如何處理您在使用 Archivists 應用程式（以下簡稱「本 App」）時的資料。我們的核心原則是：您的閱讀數據與筆記完全屬於您自己。

1. 我們收集與儲存的資料 (Data Collection and Storage)

Archivists 是一款注重隱私的本地端優先 (Local-first) 應用程式。為了讓本 App 正常運作，您所建立的所有內容，包含但不限於：

  • 書籍資料（書名、作者、ISBN 等）與自訂封面圖片
  • 閱讀紀錄與進度追蹤
  • 反思筆記 (Reflections) 與個人標籤
  • App 內的偏好設定

資料儲存位置： 上述所有資料預設均直接儲存於您的 iOS 裝置本地端。若您啟用了 iCloud 同步功能，這些資料將透過 Apple 的高安全性傳輸協定，加密備份並同步至您個人的 Apple iCloud 私有資料庫 (CloudKit Private Database) 中。我們不擁有、不租用，也不使用任何第三方伺服器來儲存您的個人資料。

2. 資料的使用與存取 (Data Usage and Access)

您儲存於 CloudKit Private Database 中的所有資料皆為完全私密：

  • 僅有您本人能透過登入相同 Apple ID 的裝置進行存取與同步。
  • 開發團隊（我們）絕對無法讀取、存取、攔截或修改您的任何個人閱讀紀錄與筆記。
  • 我們不會要求您建立 Archivists 專屬帳號，也不會收集您的姓名、電子郵件地址或電話號碼等個人識別資訊 (PII)。

3. 裝置權限的使用 (Device Permissions)

為了提供完整的核心功能體驗，本 App 可能會要求以下裝置權限，但僅限於該功能之運作：

  • 相機權限 (Camera)： 僅用於掃描書籍的 ISBN 條碼。影像畫面僅在您的裝置本地端進行即時辨識，絕對不會錄影，也不會上傳或儲存至任何外部伺服器。
  • 通知權限 (Notifications)： 僅用於提供您自行設定的「本地閱讀提醒」。這些排程皆在您的裝置端獨立運作，不會透過外部伺服器推播。

4. 第三方服務與資料共享 (Third-Party Sharing)

我們保證絕對不會將您的任何資料出售、出租或分享給任何第三方公司、廣告商或資料分析機構。本 App 內不包含任何第三方廣告追蹤程式碼 (Tracking SDK)。由於資料同步依賴 Apple 的 iCloud 服務，該部分的資料傳輸與儲存將適用於 Apple 的隱私權政策。

5. 資料的保留與刪除 (Data Retention and Deletion)

您對自己的資料擁有 100% 的控制權。您可以隨時在 App 內刪除單筆筆記或書籍。若您希望徹底刪除所有資料：

  • 您可以從 iOS 裝置上解除安裝本 App，這將刪除裝置本地的所有相關資料。
  • 若要刪除 iCloud 上的備份，請前往您的 iOS 裝置「設定」 > [您的姓名] >「iCloud」 >「管理帳號儲存空間」中，找到 Archivists 並選擇刪除資料。

6. 兒童隱私保護 (Children's Privacy)

本 App 不會刻意收集 13 歲（或當地法律規定的相關年齡）以下兒童的個人識別資訊。由於我們不收集任何使用者帳號資料，若家長或監護人發現兒童使用了本 App 並希望清除其本地數據，請直接從裝置上解除安裝本 App 即可。

7. 隱私權政策的變更 (Changes to This Policy)

我們保留隨時修改本隱私權政策的權利。任何重大變更將會在此頁面上更新，並修改頂部的「最後更新日期」。建議您定期查閱本政策以確保了解最新的隱私保護措施。
